# Air Canvas

Air Canvas lets you draw in 3D space with the iPhone rear camera. Your finished drawing and the movement used to create it can be replayed together, then exported as a silent vertical MP4.

The replay puppet is an estimated representation generated from the device's position and orientation. It is not a recording or measurement of the person's body or skeleton.

This repository contains the iOS app source, tests, required assets, and public support documents.

## Build

Open `src/AirCanvas.xcodeproj` in Xcode, select your Apple Developer Team, replace the sample bundle identifier `com.example.aircanvas` with your own, connect an ARKit-capable iPhone running iOS 18 or later, and run. Camera drawing cannot be tested in the simulator.
